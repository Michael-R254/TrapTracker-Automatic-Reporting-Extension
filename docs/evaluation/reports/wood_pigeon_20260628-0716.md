## What these figures mean

- Each of the **458 alert events** here is an alert-event count, not an animal — one alert event is one (alert rule x image) match at best-per-label; two animals in one frame register as a single event, and other species in frame, sub-threshold detections, and repeat visits are not represented (Decision 4). So this is 458 events, not 458 animals.

- Each of the **458 dated events** in the table above is placed by alert send time, not image capture time — no capture/EXIF time exists upstream (Decision 3); given the upstream ~30s email poll and ~10s inbound cadence, send-time lag is small at day granularity, except near midnight boundaries where an event may land on the adjacent day.

- Every event here (**458 events** in total) was ingested by a parser whose NOMINAL alert format was validated field-for-field against a real captured alert email on 2026-07-16 (Decision 7); edge-case variants (zero or missing attachments, malformed body, test emails) remain validated against the reconstruction only.
